# Krevo

### Cloud tools and managed websites, made simpler.

Krevo gives people and small businesses a calmer place to keep files close, share work easily, and keep their digital presence moving.

[Visit Krevo](https://krevo.io) · [Read the latest updates](./CHANGELOG.md)

## What Krevo brings together

- **Krevo Cloud** for file storage, sharing, and account management
- **A member portal** for Cloud Files, support, and billing history
- **Managed websites** for businesses that want a polished online presence without the overhead
- **Krevo Compute** for request-based access to specialized compute capacity
