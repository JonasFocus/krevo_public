# July 2026 Update

July's latest release puts Krevo Cloud at the center of the product experience. The public site now opens to Cloud, and the member portal puts Cloud Files first.

## Highlights

- Refreshed the Cloud site with clearer pricing, simpler navigation, and improved mobile layouts.
- Polished Cloud Files with a cleaner workspace, clearer status cues, and more consistent account navigation.
- Brought sign-up in line with the updated sign-in experience and improved account recovery and privacy-request paths.
- Focused the active beta on Cloud Files, support, account management, managed websites, and compute access.

## Product direction

Krevo is concentrating its current beta on a more focused Cloud experience. The public Trees page and experimental planning workspaces are no longer active product surfaces. Product updates are published here rather than in a separate public news area.

## Notes

Krevo remains proprietary, closed-source software operated as a managed service. These notes summarize customer-facing changes only; private source code, infrastructure, credentials, customer data, and sensitive implementation details are not published here.
